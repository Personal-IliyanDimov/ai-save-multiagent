# SAFe events (simplified agendas for simulation)

## PI Planning (ART) — 2 days, facilitated by the RTE
**Day 1**
1. Business context — Business Owners
2. Product vision & top features — Product Management
3. Architecture vision & dev practices — System Architect
4. Planning context & logistics — RTE
5. Team breakouts #1 — teams compute capacity/load, draft iteration plans, draft PI objectives, find risks and dependencies (PO + SM/TC + team)
6. Draft plan review — each team presents; dependencies go on the ART planning board
7. Management review & problem solving — PM, SA, BOs, RTE adjust scope/priorities

**Day 2**
1. Planning adjustments — changes from management review
2. Team breakouts #2 — Business Owners assign business value (1–10) to team PI objectives
3. Final plan review — teams present final plans
4. ART PI risks — RTE leads ROAM
5. Confidence vote (fist of five) — rework if average < 3
6. Planning retrospective

## Solution Train events — facilitated by the STE
- **Pre-Plan**: align ARTs and suppliers on capabilities before ART PI Planning.
- **Solution Train PI Planning / Post-plan**: integrate ART plans, resolve cross-ART dependencies.
- **Architect Sync**, **Solution Train Sync**: steady coordination during the PI.
- **Solution Demo**: integrated demo across ARTs and suppliers.

## ART execution events
- **ART Sync** = **PO Sync** (scope, priorities — PM + POs) + **Coach Sync** (impediments, dependencies — RTE + SMs/TCs).
- **System Demo**: every iteration, integrated increment from all teams (System Team assembles).

## Inspect & Adapt (end of each PI)
1. PI System Demo
2. Quantitative & qualitative measurement — planned vs. actual business value → ART predictability
3. Problem-solving workshop — agree problem → root cause (fishbone, 5 Whys) → Pareto → restate problem → brainstorm solutions → improvement backlog items

## Team events (SAFe Scrum)
Iteration planning · Team sync (≈15 min) · Backlog refinement · Iteration review · Iteration retrospective.
SAFe Team Kanban teams use flow-based work with the same ART cadence.

## Portfolio events
- **Strategic portfolio review** — strategy, themes, epic decisions.
- **Portfolio sync** — progress, impediments, budget.
- **Participatory budgeting** — allocate budget to value streams.
